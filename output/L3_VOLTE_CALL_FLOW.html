Gap 1, +1.16 s, and it is not the radio. At −70 to −78 dBm, Robi's INVITE still needs 1.49 s to reach UE-B. UE-B was idle. The page for that phone arrived at 1.29 s, the phone answered in the same millisecond, RRC was up at 1.38 s, and the INVITE arrived at 1.49 s. The air interface after the page is 0.20 s. The wait is before the page. Banglalink's UE-B was already connected and had the INVITE at 0.34 s. Grameenphone, on the strongest call in the drive (−60 to −69 dBm), is slower than Robi: its idle UE-B is paged at 1.67 s and receives the INVITE at 1.84 s.
Gap 2, +0.58 s. Robi starts UE-A's QCI 1 bearer at 2.05 s, after UE-B has sent 183, and that 183 takes 0.67 s to reach UE-A. Banglalink starts the bearer at 0.08 s and returns the 183 in 0.09 s. GP starts its bearer at 0.35 s.
