Gap 1, +1.84 s. Robi's INVITE needs 2.17 s to reach UE-B. UE-B was idle and read its paging occasion at 0.73 s with no page for it. The page came at the next occasion, 2.01 s, one 1.28 s paging cycle later. The terminating network got the INVITE between 0.73 s and 2.01 s, so the IMS core path takes at least 0.73 s. Banglalink's whole path to UE-B, with UE-B already connected, is 0.33 s.
Gap 2, +0.63 s. Robi starts UE-A's QCI 1 voice bearer only after UE-B's 183 comes back (2.75 s) and holds the 183 until that bearer is up. Banglalink and GP start it right after INVITE (0.08 s and 0.24 s).
